Meet Bo

Every Texas backroad has a Bigfoot story. Ours runs the store.

Bo is the big guy in the Texas cap. You will spot him on the canopy, on the sign out front and on every cup of Bo’s Coffee. Nobody has ever gotten a clear photo of him, but somebody keeps the coffee fresh, the restrooms spotless and the breakfast hot before the sun is up.

He is what a Bodega stop should feel like: friendly, a little wild and worth the exit.
